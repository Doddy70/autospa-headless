Autospa Jakarta was established 2014 in Jakarta

Passion for Detail Perfected Since 2014 Established in 2014, AutoSpa was born from a simple yet ambitious vision: to set the gold standard in the automotive detailing and paint protection industry in Indonesia. Driven by an uncompromising dedication to quality, we have grown into a highly credible, multi-branch network trusted by thousands of loyal customers.

To us, a vehicle is more than just a mode of transportation; it is a masterpiece and a valuable investment that demands flawless preservation. The extraordinary trust we have earned over the years was not built overnight, but through our relentless consistency in delivering absolute perfection—with zero tolerance for even the slightest imperfections.

A World-Class Arsenal

We understand that premium vehicles require global-standard care. Therefore, AutoSpa exclusively collaborates with and utilizes flagship products from the pioneers of the global automotive care industry:

TAC System (South Korea): Industry-leading Nano Ceramic Quartz Coating technology that provides ultimate protection, a permanent wet-look finish, and superior hydrophobic properties.

Handled by Certified Experts
The world’s finest materials mean nothing without the masterful touch of the artists who apply them. At AutoSpa, your vehicle is entrusted solely to our Certified Team of professional detailers and technicians. Having undergone rigorous international-standard training and possessing extensive hands-on experience, they ensure that every curve of the bodywork, the interior, and the engine bay receives meticulous attention. This is the embodiment of our core philosophy: Passion for Detail.

More than just an auto spa, we are the ultimate destination where automotive aesthetics, cutting-edge technology, and high-level craftsmanship converge. Entrust your prized investment to us, and experience the unparalleled pride of driving a pristine, showroom-quality vehicle every single day.
